# Einstein Moving — Sales Call Rubric v3 (Section Map)

**Effective:** rollout run for the week of 2026-09-28 (first run after this rubric ships).
**Supersedes:** `rubric_v2.md` (the Jeff Johnson / Northwood Call Map, stage/checkpoint-based) as the primary weekly grading rubric. `rubric_v2.md` and `rubric.md` both stay in the repo unchanged — v2's last score per rep is locked in as a new baseline (see **Baseline handling** below), same pattern as the v1→v2 rollout.
**Total:** 100 pts. **Passing / Green: 80+.**
**Source document:** `Einstein_15_Minute_Quote_Call_v3-9-15-26.docx`, current as of 2026-09-24 — the actual seven-section call card reps are trained on. This rubric is graded directly against that script's section structure, not a separate stage model.

---

## Why this version exists

The call script itself was substantially rewritten on 2026-09-24 (Section 1 restructured, old Section 3 split into two sections, "Two Promises" reworded, several lines reworded or dropped). The v2 rubric's Stage 1/2/3 checkpoint model no longer lines up cleanly with what's actually in the script — items like "who's coming / Meet Your Mover" don't exist in the live script at all anymore. Rather than keep patching a model that's drifted from the script, this version grades directly against the script's own seven numbered sections, so any future script edit and its rubric are the same document family and can't quietly diverge again.

**Dropped from v2, not replaced:** rapport/warmth (10 pts) and process discipline (5 pts) as standalone cross-call categories. The silence-discipline and no-repeat behaviors they covered are folded into the sections where they actually happen (see Section 4, Section 6, Section 7 below). Straight interpersonal warmth is no longer separately scored — flag if you want it reinstated as its own line; it would need to come out of one of the seven section totals below since they already sum to 100.

## Section-by-section (100 pts)

### Section 1 — OPEN (10 pts)

**Quid pro quo (5 pts)** — the highest-value single item in this section, carried over from v2 because it's still the single most load-bearing line in the call.
Recognizable version of: *"Great, it looks like we have availability within your time frame... This will take about 10 to 15 minutes, and you can walk away with a quote you can depend on... Does that sound good to you?"*
- 5 pts: framing present (time-boxed ask + promise of a dependable quote), and the rep waits for a yes before continuing
- 2 pts: framing present but rushed past — no real wait for a yes
- 0 pts: skips straight into probing with no framing at all

**Discovery basics (3 pts)** — name, moving day, city-to-city route, and approximate size, all asked.
- 3 pts: all four asked
- 1 pt: 2–3 of the four asked
- 0 pts: 0–1 asked

**Virtual walkthrough branch (2 pts)** — if the first stop is 1,800 sq ft or larger, the rep offers the 10–15 minute virtual walkthrough in this section.
- 2 pts: correctly offered when applicable, **or N/A/full credit if the home is under 1,800 sq ft** (nothing to offer)
- 0 pts: applicable (1,800 sq ft+) and not offered

### Section 2 — THEIR CONCERNS (30 pts)

This is the largest section on the card, carried over from v2's heaviest-weighted checkpoints (Their Agenda + Two Layers Deep were already 25 of v2's 40 Stage-1 points) with a new item added for the active-listening reflection this script calls out explicitly.

**Their agenda (8 pts)**
Recognizable version of: *"Other than price, what are your biggest concerns about this move, and what do you want answers to on this call?"*
- 8 pts: asked, and asked before any logistics probing starts
- 3 pts: asked, but only after probing has already started
- 0 pts: never asked

**Two layers deep (15 pts)** — heaviest single item on the whole card.
For each concern raised, did the rep go at least two layers deep (*"tell me more about that"*), continuing until the customer signals they're done, rather than one question and a pivot?
- 15 pts: real two-layer follow-up on all concerns raised
- 8 pts: attempted but shallow, or done for only some concerns
- 0 pts: concern acknowledged with no follow-up
- **15 pts (N/A/full credit) if the customer never raised any concern at all.** A follow-up gap can't exist if there was nothing to follow up on — that's not the rep's failure to score against.

**Active listening reflection (7 pts)**
Recognizable version of: *"So what I'm hearing is ________, ________ and ________. Did I get all of it?"*
- 7 pts: rep reflects back what was heard and confirms with the customer
- 3 pts: rep acknowledges concerns but doesn't reflect them back as a summary
- 0 pts: no reflection at all
- N/A/full credit if no concerns were raised (same logic as Two Layers Deep)

### Section 3 — PROBING FOR DETAILS (20 pts)

**Logistics details gathered (12 pts)** — property type, floor/stories, number of rooms, walking distance, additional rooms, special considerations, additional stops.
- 12 pts: all or nearly all of the seven items covered
- 6 pts: roughly half covered — clear gaps that would affect estimate accuracy
- 0 pts: minimal or no logistics probing beyond what Section 1 already asked

**Packing services offered (4 pts)**
Recognizable version of: *"Would you like us to pack your items into boxes for you?"*
- 4 pts: asked · 0 pts: not asked

**Box count / overflow check (4 pts)**
Recognizable version of: *"A typical box count for a home your size is ________, do you have anything like china or a book collection that might put you over a typical count?"*
- 4 pts: asked · 0 pts: not asked

### Section 4 — THE ESTIMATE (10 pts)

**Anchored, personalized estimate (4 pts)**
Gives a typical range for a home this size **and** personalizes it to something specific about this customer's move (their prep level, their stated concerns, added services).
- 4 pts: both present · 2 pts: range given, not personalized · 0 pts: no anchor at all
- **N/A/full credit (10 pts, all of Section 4) if this is a `walkthrough_scheduler` call** — a large move correctly routed to a virtual walkthrough before pricing. See **Walkthrough-scheduler calls** below.

**Pricing framing (3 pts)**
Fee transparency upfront (trip fee / overnight fee / packing materials disclosed before the customer asks) **and** the "not a guess" / 2.5-hour-minimum / 15-minute-increments commitment framing.
- 3 pts: both present · 1 pt: only one present · 0 pts: neither

**"Does that feel fair?" + real silence (3 pts)**
Asked, and followed by an actual pause — no immediate re-pitch or filling the silence.
- 3 pts: asked and silence held · 1 pt: asked but rep talks over the silence · 0 pts: not asked

### Section 5 — TWO PROMISES (10 pts)

**On-Time Guarantee delivered (4 pts)**
Covers the discount terms (starts discounting immediately if late) in recognizable form.
- 4 pts: fully delivered · 2 pts: mentioned but vague on terms · 0 pts: not covered

**No Worries Damage Coverage delivered (4 pts)**
Covers the $2,000 coverage, no deductible / no fine print framing, and that it's built into the rate (not an add-on).
- 4 pts: fully delivered · 2 pts: mentioned but missing the no-deductible/no-fine-print framing · 0 pts: not covered

**Closes the loop (2 pts)**
Asks a version of *"Does that put those to rest?"* and doesn't repeat anything already fully covered earlier in the call.
- 2 pts: both · 0 pts: neither, or a clear repeat of something already answered

### Section 6 — CONCERNS AGAIN (10 pts)

**Genuine final concerns check (5 pts)**
Recognizable version of: *"What's still concerning you about this move?"* — not the empty "any questions?" that gets an automatic no.
- 5 pts: genuine version asked · 2 pts: asked as "any questions?" or equivalent throwaway · 0 pts: not asked

**Follow-up on remaining concern (3 pts)**
If the customer raises something here, does the rep dig in (*"tell me more about that"*) rather than brushing past it?
- 3 pts: real follow-up · 0 pts: acknowledged, no follow-up
- N/A/full credit if nothing was raised at this check

**Catch-all asked (2 pts)**
Recognizable version of: *"Anything coming with you we haven't talked about?"*
- 2 pts: asked · 0 pts: not asked

### Section 7 — ASK FOR THE BUSINESS (10 pts)

**Explicit ask, no easy out (5 pts)**
Recognizable version of: *"Would you like our help with your move?"* — asked plainly, never softened into an out like *"or I can just email this over."*
- 5 pts: asked plainly · 2 pts: asked but with a built-in out · 0 pts: never asked

**Rate-lock / no-deposit mentioned (2 pts)**
- 2 pts: mentioned · 0 pts: not mentioned

**Booking confirmed with details (3 pts)**
Date, phone number, arrival window, crew size, addresses confirmed, and notes left for the crew about what the customer was worried about.
- 3 pts: fully confirmed · 1 pt: partially confirmed (missing 2+ items) · 0 pts: booking details not confirmed at all

## N/A rule

Same convention as prior rubric versions: if a bullet's underlying situation doesn't apply to this call, full credit — don't penalize for a scenario that never came up. Explicit N/A carve-outs are called out per item above (Section 2's Two Layers Deep and Active Listening, Section 6's Follow-up on remaining concern, and the walkthrough-scheduler override on Section 4). Every other item applies to every call and does not get an automatic N/A.

## Walkthrough-scheduler calls

Some intake calls correctly route to a virtual walkthrough before any pricing happens — large moves (≥1,800 sq ft) where the script itself (Section 1) directs the rep to schedule the walkthrough instead of quoting on this call. Mark these `call_type: "walkthrough_scheduler"`.

**Scoring rule:** if the call properly and cleanly routes to the walkthrough (the rep didn't abandon the call mid-pitch, didn't get cut off, actually confirmed next steps), score **Sections 4, 5, 6, and 7 as N/A/full credit** (10 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 40 pts) — none of that was supposed to happen on this call. Sections 1, 2, and 3 (Open including the walkthrough offer, Their Concerns, Probing for Details) still score normally — discovery and detail-gathering still apply and still matter on a walkthrough-routed call.

**Do NOT apply this N/A treatment** if the call trails off, gets transferred without a clean handoff, or abandons the pitch mid-stream for reasons other than a genuine walkthrough routing — that's a real 0 on whichever sections were skipped, not a deferred credit.

## Baseline handling

Every rep's most recent score under v2 (`rubric_v2.md`) is locked in as a v2 baseline, the same way v1 scores were locked in when v2 launched. Starting with the rollout run, all grading uses v3; going forward, reps are compared against their own v3 trajectory, not the v2 number — the two scales don't convert to each other, since the point structure and section boundaries are different, not just reweighted.
