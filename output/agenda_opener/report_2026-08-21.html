Broader read — did naming a concern actually lead anywhere? Across 70 calls, a customer named a real concern or worry on 43 of them. Of those, 17 turned into a genuine back-and-forth (the rep asked real follow-up questions and explored it) and 26 were acknowledged and dropped (rep logged it or gave a one-line answer, then moved straight to the next topic). So the strict "emotion follow-up" phrasing undercounts real engagement — several reps (Chauntelle, Brianne, Jules, Zhang) are having real conversations about concerns, just not in the specific "tell me more" / "what happened" language the narrow rubric item looks for. The gap is smaller than the 0% headline suggests, but still means fewer than half of named concerns (17/43) get real follow-through.
