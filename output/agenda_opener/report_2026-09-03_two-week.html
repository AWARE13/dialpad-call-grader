Two things worth your call before this gets used for coaching

Methodology judgment call, not yet confirmed: on several Zhang Pammit calls, the grader treated a quick move-date/home-type check before the formal opener as acceptable triage, not disqualifying "asked before probing." A stricter reading (any pre-opener question at all counts as probing) would flip roughly half of her "before probing" results from true to false. Worth deciding with Tetet which reading matches the rubric's intent — it moves her ranking either way.
Not-applicable rate: 56 of 375 pulled calls (15%) were reschedules, existing-booking service calls, claims/damage follow-ups, or (in one case) an unusable transcript — consistent with the skip rate the main weekly grader sees. Not a data problem, just the real mix of what CET reps answer the phone for.
