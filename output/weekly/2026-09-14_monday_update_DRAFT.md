**Two things to know before anyone quotes these numbers:**

1. **Email Handoff reads 9% this week against 69–95% on every run since June.**
   That is a definition change, not a drop in performance. This run required the
   rubric's actual "reply with any corrections" verification ask; earlier runs
   credited any mention of sending a confirmation email. Reps did not change what
   they are doing — the bar moved. The all-time number for that row now mixes two
   definitions and should not be cited until we settle it.

2. **The walkthrough-scheduler calls are not comparable to each other,** because
   the mini-rubric for them is still marked PENDING in our own rubric file. Four
   were scored across all nine sections, one was auto-credited on the sections
   that "don't apply" per the older convention — a 45-point spread on the same
   call type. They are excluded from every headline number above. This needs a
   decision from us before those scores mean anything.

**What I take from this run.** Everything except Save Your Ass sits in the 60–70%
band — the team knows the material. The score is not moving because the misses
are concentrated in the two sections that require initiative rather than recall:
the prep speech and the booking ask. That is a coaching problem, not a content
problem, and it is the same conclusion as last month. The 60-minute calibration
session with Nhel and Tetet is the unblock, and both methodology items above
belong on that agenda.
